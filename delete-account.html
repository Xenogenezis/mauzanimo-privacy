Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your MauZanimo account and the data linked to it. MauZanimo is the pet adoption app for Mauritius published on Google Play.

Option 1: delete it in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the MauZanimo app and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom bar.
  3. Open Account & privacy and tap Delete my account.
  4. Read the summary, type DELETE to confirm, and tap Continue.
  5. Confirm it's you: enter your password, or sign in with Google again.

Your account is deleted straight away. Tip: use Download my data in the same menu first if you want a copy.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you can't sign in or no longer have the app, email zanimomau@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my MauZanimo account", from the email address you used to register. We'll confirm the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address and phone number
  • Your sign-in account
  • Pets you put up for adoption
  • Your lost & found posts and street sightings
  • Your saved pets, success stories and pet updates
  • Your impact history (your leaderboard entry is hidden)
  • Your event registrations are cancelled

What is kept, and for how long

Adoption applications, foster requests, adoption inquiries and rescue reports are kept for animal welfare follow-up, so the team can check on animals that were adopted or rescued. They are visible only to the MauZanimo team. You can ask us to remove them at any time by emailing zanimomau@gmail.com, and we'll remove them within 30 days.

For more details, read our privacy policy.

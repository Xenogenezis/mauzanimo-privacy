Privacy Policy

Powered by JCI Grand Baie

Last updated: April 2026

MauZanimo is a free digital pet adoption platform developed by JCI Grand Baie, Mauritius. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we collect and use your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use MauZanimo, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and phone number when you register
  • Pet listing information: Photos, descriptions, and location details you provide when listing a pet
  • Adoption inquiry information: Your name, email, phone number, and message when you submit an adoption inquiry
  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with the app, including pages visited and features used
  • Device information: Basic device information such as operating system and app version

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Connect pet adopters with pet owners and rescue organisations
  • Process and manage adoption inquiries
  • Send notifications about your adoption inquiries and account activity
  • Improve the app and user experience
  • Communicate updates about MauZanimo and JCI Grand Baie initiatives

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With pet owners and adopters: Contact details shared as part of the adoption process with your consent
  • With partner organisations: Veterinary clinics and animal shelters listed on MauZanimo may receive booking and inquiry details
  • With Firebase (Google): We use Google Firebase for authentication, database, and storage services
  • As required by law: If required by legal obligation or to protect the rights and safety of our users

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers located in Europe. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below.

6. Children's Privacy

MauZanimo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Third Party Services

MauZanimo uses the following third party services:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, database, and cloud storage
  • Google Sign-In: Optional login via Google account
  • WhatsApp: Optional direct messaging feature

These services have their own privacy policies which we encourage you to review.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of MauZanimo after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • Organisation: JCI Grand Baie
  • Email: mauzanimo@jcigrandbaie.mu
  • Location: Grand Baie, Mauritius

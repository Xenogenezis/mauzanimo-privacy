Privacy Policy

Rehome responsibly. Adopt locally.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

MauZanimo is a free community app that helps rehome animals in Mauritius. This page explains what personal data the app collects, why, who can see it, and your rights under the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, phone number (optional), your role in the app and your sign-in details (email and password, or Google Sign-In).
  • Content you post: pets you put up for adoption, lost & found reports, street sightings, success stories, pet updates, photos and descriptions.
  • Location: only the place name or GPS position you choose to attach to a post or sighting. The app does not track your location in the background.
  • Activity: pets you save, adoption and foster applications, adoption inquiries and their messages, event registrations, rescue reports and impact points.

We do not use advertising, analytics or advertising identifiers.

2. Why we use it

To run the app: show animals for adoption, put adopters in touch with people rehoming a pet, reunite lost pets with their owners, follow up adoptions for the animals' welfare, organise events, manage your account, and keep the community safe from abuse and scams.

3. Who can see it

  • Public posts: pet listings, lost & found posts, sightings and stories are public. Anyone using the app can see them, including the photo, location and any contact number you enter in the post.
  • Private information: your profile, adoption and foster applications and inquiries are visible only to you and the MauZanimo team.
  • Service provider: our data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (authentication, database and storage) on our behalf.
  • The law: we may disclose information if we are legally required to.

We do not sell your personal data or share it with advertisers.

4. Photos

Photos taken with a phone can contain hidden details such as where they were taken. Avoid posting photos that show your home address or other people's faces.

5. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored on Google Firebase. Access to private data is restricted by security rules, and only the MauZanimo team can see applications and reports.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile and posts are erased. Adoption, foster, inquiry and rescue records are kept for animal welfare follow-up and can be removed on request. See how to delete your account.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act 2017 you have the right to:

  • Access your data: in the app, Profile > Account & privacy > Download my data
  • Rectify it: edit your profile or your posts
  • Erase it: Profile > Account & privacy > Delete my account (full steps)
  • Object to processing, and complain to the Data Protection Office of Mauritius

8. Children

MauZanimo is intended for adults (18 and over). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact the MauZanimo team at zanimomau@gmail.com.
